## Full description

Keep track of the books you own and the books you lend.

Carribrary is the Android companion for Carribrary Server. Before using it, install the server on your own Windows computer, Linux desktop or Raspberry Pi and pair your phone using a private QR code. A hosted library service is not included. Server downloads and setup instructions: [PUBLIC DOWNLOADS URL].

• Scan a book's ISBN/EAN barcode or enter it manually. Look up available title, author, publisher and cover information, then assign each physical copy its own catalog number.
• Search your collection, view covers, sort by title, author, series or catalog number, and choose how many books appear on each page.
• Keep borrower names and optional phone numbers or email addresses.
• Check out a particular copy, set an optional due date, and record its return.
• Prepare a borrowing receipt in your text or email app. You review it and tap Send.
• Share a read-only book list by link or QR code when public sharing is configured on your server. Friends can submit borrowing requests for you to review.
• Edit books, remove borrowers, review loan history, manage paired phones, export backups and choose library colors from your phone.

Your household server stores the master library. The phone can display its cached library while offline; an active server connection is needed to make changes. Scanning runs on the phone. Book metadata and covers require internet access and depend on the information available from their providers.

Use your home network for local access. Access away from home requires the server's secure remote connection setup. Public sharing also requires its separate HTTPS setup. You do not need ChatGPT hosting or Tailscale.
