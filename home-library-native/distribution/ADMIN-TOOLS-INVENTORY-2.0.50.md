# Administrator tool inventory — 2.0.50

All administration functions from the [2.0.47 inventory](ADMIN-TOOLS-INVENTORY-2.0.47.md) remain available.

| Tool | Previous location | New location |
|---|---|---|
| Analytics & insights (server/iPhone PWA) | Expandable Dashboard section | Settings & Tools → Data & History → Analytics & insights; route `#settings/data-history/library-insights` |
| Analytics & insights (Android) | Home button | Settings & Tools → Data & History → Analytics & insights |

The settings card participates in settings search. Server/PWA analytics uses a genuine page with breadcrumbs and Back navigation, without an expandable dashboard container. Existing reader statistics stay in Reading Tracker / My reading. Other cards, dashboard Quick Actions, pairing, permissions, backups and data are unchanged.

## Unified notification bell

Server/PWA header and native Android banner open the Admin notifications inbox. Account approvals link to People & Access → Borrower accounts; pending books to Book requests; renewals to Borrower renewals; approved/ready holds to Lending & holds; overdue loans to Books on loan. Recovery review retains its existing local safeguards; backup, reminder and push problems link to their existing status/settings tools. The bell counts action items, not just book requests, and reading summaries remain separate. Nothing was removed or relocated from Settings & Tools by this change.

## Reader acquisition suggestions

Books to buy remains reachable from Dashboard Quick Actions and Books & Organization. Its new Reader purchase suggestions section supports considering, declining and confirmed deletion on server/PWA and Android. Pending suggestions also link here from the bell. Public shared catalog and approved borrower catalog offer Suggest a book or series to buy. These do not expose or alter the private acquisition wishlist or personal TBR. All prior tools remain in place.
