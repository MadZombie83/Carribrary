Data & privacy

Help with my account

Creating an account is optional: the shared link lets you browse, request books and suggest a book or series for the librarian to buy without signing in. An account adds a private TBR list and reading tracker. Approved accounts can also use Suggest a book or series to buy next to Catalog filters. Purchase suggestions go to librarians, are not linked to accounts or encrypted personal lists, and do not reserve a book or guarantee a purchase. Do not include contact details or private notes; ask the librarian if you want a suggestion removed.

Catalog opens first. Search by title, author, series, ISBN or copy ID; filter by Collection, sort by Title/Author/Series/Catalog number, and choose 10, 25 or 50 per page. These controls remain available after sign-in, even with your private list locked. Use My reading for TBR and reading history, My loans for borrowing history and requests, and Account for these help, device and privacy settings. Private-list lock/unlock, wishlist and encrypted backups are under Account → Private reading & wishlist. To cancel a borrowing request, open My loans → My book requests.

Your personal reading, TBR, notes and private wishlist use a separate private passphrase, different from your sign-in password. Save the recovery code during setup. The librarian cannot unlock the encrypted personal list or recover it without your code. Catalog and borrowing work while it is locked. Use the private-list controls to clear finished reading or export/restore an encrypted personal backup. The server delivers this web app, so encryption is not protection against a malicious host changing the app code. Older backups may contain earlier unencrypted records; current legacy records are encrypted during setup.

About this book opens a short description when available. Descriptions may come from the librarian, Open Library or Google Books; the source is shown. Your private reading notes are separate.

Due-date notifications are optional: sign in to an approved account, add the page to your Home Screen, then tap Enable due-date notifications. Allow permission only if you want reminders. Turn them off on this device with the same button. iPhone needs iOS 16.4 or later and the Home-screen app. Email and tap-to-send texts remain separate. Notifications are generic; open your account for book details. The server must stay on; delivery depends on browser/OS settings.

The shared catalog, sign-in, account and installed borrower pages use the library’s saved colors, rounded cards and consistent headings. Signed-in borrower pages reuse the librarian dashboard layout with borrower-only navigation. On a phone, open the menu for the full branded sidebar; Close, Escape or tapping outside closes it. Your Catalog overview shows your current loans, due dates and holds. Enabled Self-service actions appear below the overview. Catalog still opens first. Available, On hold and On loan badges show each copy’s current state. Catalog overview counts refer to visible books; My loans counts refer only to your own loans and requests. Account groups the same private-list, device, data/privacy, help and sign-out controls. Refresh an open page after the librarian changes the appearance.

Registration uses attempt limits. Some libraries also enable Cloudflare Turnstile verification; complete it if shown. If verification is unavailable, try later or contact the librarian. After signing up, the librarian must approve and link your account before your history is shown. Catalog opens first after sign-in. My reading is second and opens your To Be Read (TBR) list: Want to read and Currently reading. Add a catalog book with Add to TBR / track reading. Mark as read removes it from TBR while preserving dates, ratings and notes under Read / finished or All reading history. Each person has their own list; a TBR entry does not reserve, request or check out a book. Use Catalog to ask for a book; only the librarian can check books out or mark them returned. Requesting an available physical copy places it on hold pending approval. Approval does not start a loan; checkout starts when the librarian confirms handover. Use Cancel request under My book requests, review the book and confirm cancellation. Keep request makes no change. A conflict message stays visible after refresh; review the current state before confirming again. Closing a request releases only its associated hold. Refresh updates requests under My loans and the open-request count in navigation. Clearing history is not required to cancel or decline. Reading status is separate from loans. Forgot your sign-in password? Use Forgot your password? for email recovery if enabled, or ask the librarian for a one-time reset link. This does not unlock your private reading list. Request more time for an active dated loan under My loans → Request renewal; approval and limits follow library policy. Sign out when using a shared device. For installation use the public HTTPS address with a trusted certificate in a regular browser. A local http:// network address or Incognito may allow only a shortcut. Create shortcut alone does not mean the app is installed. The plain welcome page does not offer installation. Use Account → This device to install the borrower app. Close old tabs, reopen the HTTPS link and refresh after updating the server; no account or browser data needs to be cleared. To save this borrower app on Android, tap Install / add to Home screen, or Chrome’s ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen → Install. No APK or private phone-pairing QR is needed. The server must stay reachable. Installing does not grant extra access. For finished private reading use Clear private finished reading in the encrypted-list controls. For completed loans/requests, open Account → Data & privacy → My data & privacy → Clear completed history. Active loans, holds and unfinished reading stay protected. To delete your sign-in account or linked personal information, open Account → Data & privacy → My data & privacy → Delete my account. Review the preview, enter your password and type the sign-in username shown in the preview, not your display name. The borrower field ignores capitalization, surrounding spaces and one optional @; passwords must still match exactly. Both scopes delete your current encrypted vault and sign-in access. Export your encrypted personal backup first if wanted. Account-only deletion keeps operational library records; full information deletion requires resolving active loans first. The librarian also has deletion tools. Old exports, emails and offline copies may still contain older information.
