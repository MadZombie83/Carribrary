Help with my account

Catalog opens first. Use My reading for TBR and reading history, My loans for borrowing history and requests, and Account for these help, device and privacy settings. Private-list lock/unlock, wishlist and encrypted backups are under Account → Private reading & wishlist. To cancel a borrowing request, open My loans → My book requests.

Your personal reading, TBR, notes and private wishlist use a separate private passphrase, different from your sign-in password. Save the recovery code during setup. The librarian cannot unlock the encrypted personal list or recover it without your code. Catalog and borrowing work while it is locked. Use the private-list controls to clear finished reading or export/restore an encrypted personal backup. The server delivers this web app, so encryption is not protection against a malicious host changing the app code. Older backups may contain earlier unencrypted records; current legacy records are encrypted during setup.

About this book opens a short description when available. Descriptions may come from the librarian, Open Library or Google Books; the source is shown. Your private reading notes are separate.

Due-date notifications are optional: sign in to an approved account, add the page to your Home Screen, then tap Enable due-date notifications. Allow permission only if you want reminders. Turn them off on this device with the same button. iPhone needs iOS 16.4 or later and the Home-screen app. Email and tap-to-send texts remain separate. Notifications are generic; open your account for book details. The server must stay on; delivery depends on browser/OS settings.

The shared catalog, sign-in, account and installed borrower pages use the library’s saved colors. Refresh an open page after the librarian changes them.

Registration uses attempt limits. Some libraries also enable Cloudflare Turnstile verification; complete it if shown. If verification is unavailable, try later or contact the librarian. After signing up, the librarian must approve and link your account before your history is shown. Catalog opens first after sign-in. My reading is second and opens your To Be Read (TBR) list: Want to read and Currently reading. Add a catalog book with Add to TBR / track reading. Mark as read removes it from TBR while preserving dates, ratings and notes under Read / finished or All reading history. Each person has their own list; a TBR entry does not reserve, request or check out a book. Use Catalog to ask for a book; only the librarian can check books out or mark them returned. Requesting an available physical copy places it on hold pending approval. Approval does not start a loan; checkout starts when the librarian confirms handover. Use Cancel request under My book requests, review the book and confirm cancellation. Keep request makes no change. A conflict message stays visible after refresh; review the current state before confirming again. Closing a request releases only its associated hold. Refresh updates requests under My loans and the open-request count in navigation. Clearing history is not required to cancel or decline. Reading status is separate from loans. Forgot your password? Ask the librarian for a one-time reset link. Sign out when using a shared device. To save this borrower app on Android, tap Install / add to Home screen, or Chrome’s ⋮ menu → Add to Home screen → Install. No APK or private phone-pairing QR is needed. The server must stay reachable. Installing does not grant extra access. For finished private reading use Clear private finished reading in the encrypted-list controls. For completed loans/requests, open Account → Data & privacy → My data & privacy → Clear completed history. Active loans, holds and unfinished reading stay protected. To delete your sign-in account or linked personal information, open Account → Data & privacy → My data & privacy → Delete my account. Review the preview, enter your password and type the sign-in username shown in the preview, not your display name. The borrower field ignores capitalization, surrounding spaces and one optional @; passwords must still match exactly. Both scopes delete your current encrypted vault and sign-in access. Export your encrypted personal backup first if wanted. Account-only deletion keeps operational library records; full information deletion requires resolving active loans first. The librarian also has deletion tools. Old exports, emails and offline copies may still contain older information.
